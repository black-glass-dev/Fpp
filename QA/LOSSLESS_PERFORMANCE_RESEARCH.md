## Primary documentation reviewed and decisions
1. Unity2022.3 SRP Batcher: https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/Manual/SRPBatcher.html . Reduces CPU preparation/dispatch overhead for matching shader variants; not proof of fewer drawn triangles or actual instancing. Check real Frame Debugger/recorder output. Existing custom shader has UnityPerMaterial CBUFFER already: do not "fix" a missing CBUFFER that isn't missing.
2. Unity2022.3 FrameTimingManager: https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/Manual/frame-timing-manager.html . Native timings arrive with delay; zero GPU time is unavailable data, not a fast GPU. CPU total includes waiting. Preserve wall-clock p95/max and export native CPU main/render/present/GPU only when genuinely available.
3. Unity FrameTiming API: https://docs.unity3d.com/ScriptReference/FrameTiming.html . Do not infer CPU/GPU bottleneck from a screenshot or enabled scene triangle count. Present waiting/caps can dominate CPU frame duration. Verify2022.3 binding fields against UnityCsReference Runtime/Export/Graphics/Graphics.bindings.cs and PlayerSettings.bindings.cs.
4. Unity mip streaming: https://docs.unity3d.com/2021.3/Documentation/Manual/TextureStreaming.html and https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/manual/materials-and-shaders/textures/texture-loading/texture-streaming/configure . Low memory budgets CAN lower visible mip quality/pop. Not a free lossless switch. Any streaming implementation must preserve original full mip chains, meet visible mip demand and test rapid turns/close approach; no silently lowered mip budget or resolution. Current glTF textures are embedded non-streaming subassets; a global toggle alone does not stream them.
5. Unity6 GPU Resident Drawer: https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/urp/gpu-resident-drawer.html . Requires Forward+, compute support, MeshRenderer; excludes OpenGL ES. Shader/BRG compatibility and API/device matter. It targets rendering submission; it does not make unique8K textures smaller or remove excessive geometry. Unity6 upgrade is an optional controlled same-content comparison, not a promised performance fix. Keep GLES fallback and camera/hand/shader/render gates intact.
6. Unity CPU profiling: https://docs.unity3d.com/2018.4/Documentation/Manual/ProfilerCPU.html . WaitForPresent can mean GPU, render-thread work or presentation waiting; inspect render-thread timeline and GPU together rather than labeling all main-thread duration script cost.
